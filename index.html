Proving negligence after an accident

In Kentucky, everyone owes each other a reasonable duty of care. When someone fails to uphold that duty, they may be held liable for an accident. Our lawyers know what it takes to prove negligence in a personal injury lawsuit.

Support after the death of a loved one

When your loss was caused by another person’s negligence, some families choose to file a wrongful death lawsuit. Loss of income, medical expenses, and funeral and burial costs can all be recovered through a wrongful death claim.
